HazardPath footer refinement

Research reviewed 26 October 2026 using Perplexity source search.

Directly supported findings

Nielsen Norman Group: Footers 101 explains that people deliberately seek contact, company and utility information at the bottom of a page. Grouping improves scanability; footer navigation is useful after long pages. The implemented footer follows this guidance with visible link groups rather than a collapsed mobile directory.

NN/g menu-design checklist emphasizes predictable locations and readable contrast for navigation. The footer uses dark text on pale blue and a dedicated legal row.

Sector context

Watershed’s platform provides an enterprise sustainability positioning reference. Linear’s security page is a reference for giving trust information a clear destination. These were text-source reviews, not pixel-level footer audits, and no visual layout is attributed to these companies.

Our design choices

  • A pale sky background gives the end of the page a distinct boundary while retaining HazardPath’s light palette.
  • Four navigation groups: Products, Platform, Resources and Company.
  • Contour branding and an editorial closing statement give the footer a company-specific identity.
  • Contact, demo, access, trust, accessibility and policy destinations remain visible.
  • No invented social accounts, office addresses, certification badges or subscription forms.
  • Two-column navigation on small screens, visible keyboard focus, and a back-to-top link.
